This is a placeholder post. Delete it once you write something real.

Posts live in `_posts/` and are named `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`. The block between the
two `---` lines is the front matter: it sets the title, date, and optional
description, tags, and categories. Everything below it is plain Markdown.

Math works out of the box with `$$ ... $$`:

$$ E = mc^2 $$

So do code blocks, images via the `figure` include, footnotes, and callouts. To
keep a post as a draft, set `published: false` in the front matter.
